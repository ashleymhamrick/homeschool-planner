What are the photos of?

Claude lists the lessons in order. The planner puts them on your school days and skips break weeks. It does not write steps, because a table of contents does not say how to teach. You can add steps later.

  1. 1. PhotosTake photos of one week of lessons in your teacher's book.
  2. 2. Claude readsClaude puts each lesson in your day. It writes the steps.
  3. 3. You checkRead the list and fix errors. Then add it to your planner.

Take one page at a time, or choose several from your photos. Each new photo is added to the list.
